How to read this table

Bucks County Health Department licenses sewage hauler vehicles annually; this list is the approved sewage haulers roster for 2024–2025, revised 7 July 2025. DEP 5-digit transporter numbers are not on this PDF — marked unknown. Confirm on PA DEP’s residential septage hauler registration page.

Three Waste Management location rows (Morrisville, Primos, Tullytown) have no phone printed — marked unknown. Franc Environmental Inc. prints “215-443-0650 / Wind River”; we record the phone only. L & C Septic Pumping LLC ZIP is printed as 1807 (truncated; not expanded). Multiple Waste Management rows with different addresses are separate records as printed.
