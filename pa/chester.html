How to read this table

CCHD licenses liquid waste pumpers under Chapter 504. The CCHD Nr column is the county license as printed (LWH-*). Phones and addresses are as printed — including odd spellings such as Plumsteaville and the backtick in Ink`s.

Pennsylvania also requires DEP registration for residential septage haulers. DEP transporter numbers are not on this county PDF, so they are not invented here.

A liquid waste pumper license is not a septic inspection credential. Pumping a tank is not a full inspection. How often to pump · Inspection before sale.

The PDF says data is subject to change; call CCHD at 610-344-6688 with questions, and confirm current licensure before you hire.
