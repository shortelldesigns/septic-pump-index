Transcribed from McLean County Health Department’s PDF 2026 List of Septic System Pumpers, revised 12 May 2026 (0601-30-Pumper-list-2026). The county licenses individuals who may pump septic systems. We did not add companies from business directories.

Source retrieved 5 September 2026 (US/Pacific). Official file: DocumentCenter/View/23368/0601-30-Pumper-list-2026. Parent page: Septic Systems Contractor Information. Statewide context: Illinois Department of Public Health.

How to read this table

This is a list of licensed pumpers (people), not a firm-only roster. Several people can share one business name. Phone numbers shown are the Business line from the PDF; mobile and fax lines were not copied into the table.

Higgs, William R is printed NOT FOR HIRE on the source (Wild Wood Camp Ground). We keep that row so the transcription matches the PDF, but homeowners should not treat it as an available contractor.

A pumper license is not an installer credential. McLean County publishes a separate licensed-installers PDF. Pumping a tank is not a full inspection. How often to pump · Inspection before sale.
