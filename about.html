About Septic Pump Index

A U.S. directory for homeowners who need a licensed septic pumper or inspector. We publish official county and state lists and EPA/extension how-to. We do not invent records.

First version published 29 August 2026 (US/Pacific).

What we are

Septic Pump Index helps you find a pumper or inspector a government list actually names — then understand pumping frequency, sale-time inspection, and failure signs from EPA and university extension. It is not a government website, not a hauler, and not a real-estate inspector.

Methodology

  • Listing rows are transcribed from official county or state PDFs or agency pages we fetched. Each JSON record carries a source_url and document date.
  • If a field is not on the source (DEP transporter number, DNR license number, Ohio bond, prices, which towns they serve), we write unknown or omit it and link the official tool.
  • We do not copy phone numbers from Google or marketplace sites. We do not “enrich” records.
  • We do not rank “best septic company.” Sort order is the source’s order or alphabetic as printed.
  • Guidance pages quote or closely paraphrase EPA and Penn State Extension, with page dates. We skipped stale dollar figures from older EPA brochures.
  • A source that is years out of date is logged and not presented as a current roster. Example: Wisconsin DNR SeptageBusinessList.pdf footer “Report run on: 6/30/2023.”

How to add a county: see README.md. What we fetched: SOURCES.md.
