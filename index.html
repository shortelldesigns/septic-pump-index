1. Pump

A registered hauler empties the tank

EPA: household tanks are typically pumped every three to five years. Frequency depends on tank size, household size, water use, and solids. How often, with citations.

2. Inspect

Inspection is a different job

EPA: in many states a septic system must be inspected when real estate transfers. Routine inspections should continue every 1 to 3 years. Pumping a tank is not a full inspection. Before you buy or sell.

3. Repair

Repair often needs a different license

In Ohio, installer, service provider, and septage hauler are separate local registrations. A backup or wet drainfield is not a DIY weekend. Warning signs, from EPA.

How-to (official sources)

  • How often to pump — EPA 3–5 years, plus Penn State Extension on 2–3 years and Pennsylvania municipal rules.
  • Inspection before sale — EPA homebuyer guide on what an inspection should cover. Local rules vary; we do not invent them.
  • Warning signs — backups, wet spots, odors, lush grass in dry weather. When EPA says to call a professional.

What this site will not do

We do not invent companies, phone numbers, license numbers, prices, or county pumping rules. If a field is not on the official list, it is marked unknown or omitted, and we link the official tool. We do not rank “best septic company.” There are no paid placements. Call links appear only where the official list already printed a number.
