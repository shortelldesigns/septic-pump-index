How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Trumbull County septage-hauler registration only. Local REG # and ODH bond status are unknown on this PDF (no registration numbers printed).

The PDF title is SEPTAGE HAULERS 2026 (CreationDate 21 April 2026 PDT). We list all 28 named rows.
