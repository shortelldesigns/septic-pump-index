Transcribed from Darke County General Health District’s PDF Septage Haulers (SEWAGE-TREATMENT-04-1-2026HAULERS.pdf, footer 1 April 2026). Companies that haul septage in Darke County must register with the health district. We did not add companies from business directories.

Source retrieved 24 September 2026 (US/Pacific). Official file: SEWAGE-TREATMENT-04-1-2026HAULERS.pdf. Parent page: Sewage Contractor Registrations. Program: Sewage Treatment Systems. Verify statewide bonds via ODH Information for Contractors.

How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Darke County septage-hauler registration only. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The PDF prints 20 TOTAL (footer 04/01/2026 on each page). Spellings are as printed (including BOOSO'S, FLATTER'S, FRECH'S, MIKE'S). ARTZ SEPTIC SERVICES phone is kept as printed (1-459-996-6 — truncated on the PDF text layer); street line prints GREENVILLE NASHVILLE with no Rd/St suffix. MT SERVICES INC operator line prints MILLER PORTABLES; PORTA KLEEN operator line prints singular SERVICE. No registration numbers appear on this PDF.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Butler County haulers · Clark County haulers.
