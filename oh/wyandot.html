How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Wyandot County septage-hauler registration only. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The PDF prints 10 TOTAL (footer 09/21/2026, Page 1). Spellings are as printed, including the DBA lines MR. FIXALL HANDYMAN SERVICES, LLC DBA MR. FIX and MT SERVICES INC DBA MILLER PORTABLES. MT SERVICES INC DBA MILLER PORTABLES has no operator name line (marked unknown). SMOKEY'S MINI LAKE CAMPGROUNDS phone is printed - - - (marked unknown; no number was invented). Street and city lines are joined with a space, as printed, with no added comma between them. No local registration numbers appear on this PDF. Wyandot County Public Health: 127-A SOUTH SANDUSKY AVENUE, UPPER SANDUSKY, OH 43351, phone 1-419-294-3852, fax 1-419-294-6424.

The Home Sewage Treatment topic page still links an older Approved Septic Haulers file, SEWAGE-TREATMENT-04-24-2025.pdf (footer 04/24/2025, 11 TOTAL). This table does not transcribe that older PDF, and it does not merge the installer or service-provider lists on that page.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Seneca County haulers · Marion County haulers.
