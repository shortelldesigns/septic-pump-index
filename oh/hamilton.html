How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Hamilton County septage-hauler registration only. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF. Operator is unknown (company, address, city, phone, and local registration number only).

The PDF footer prints 11/7/2026 2:00:34PM and Page 1 of 1. We list all 31 named rows. Spellings are as printed, including 4070LAWRENCEBURG (no space), 1780 STATE ROUTE (no route number), AAA WASTEWATER-DBA, PROKLEEN INDUSTRIAL SERVICES INS DBA PORTA KLEEN, FAIRFIELD DRIVE, 45014, UNITED RENTALS ( NORTH AMERICA) (space after the opening parenthesis), BLANCHESTER OHIO, and CINCINNATI, OH, 45211. The company column runs into the address column on PORTA KLEEN, RELIABLE ONSITE SERVICES, and TRENTEL'S PORTABLES; Rumpke's EXT 7080 is printed across HSR0000035. Those strings are kept with the field they belong to. Hamilton County Public Health phone 513-946-7800.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Pumping a tank is not an inspection. The same parent page also links registered installers and service providers — those are separate credentials and are not merged into this hauler table.
