How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Williams County septage-hauler registration only. It is local registration, not the ODH statewide bond list. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The PDF prints 8 TOTAL (footer 02/19/2026, Page 1). Spellings are as printed. AUTOMATIC SEPTIC & WELL, CO. keeps the comma and CO. FISCHER PLUMBING & HEATING INC has no period after INC; the operator line is printed FISCHER PLUMBING AND HEATING (AND, not an ampersand). LAYMAN SEPTIC TANK & DRAIN CLEANING LLC keeps the address 18-408 CTY RD 20 as printed, including the hyphen. PRECISION WELL AND SEPTIC, LLC keeps AND and the comma before LLC. UNITED RENTALS (NORTH AMERICA) INC has no comma or period after INC; the second line is printed DBA: RELIABLE ONSITE SERVICES and is kept in the operator column. TRESSLER PLUMBING LLC is printed with address BOX 433, not PO BOX. Street and city lines are joined with a space, as printed, with no added comma between them. No local registration numbers appear on this PDF. Phones are shown as printed. The official file is a scan of the printed roster (no text layer). Williams County Health Department: 310 LINCOLN AVE, MONTPELIER, OH 43543, phone 1-419-485-3141, fax 1-419-485-5420.

The Household Sewage Treatment Systems page also links Installers and Service Provider PDFs. This table does not transcribe those PDFs, and it does not add firms from the ODH bond list.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Van Wert County haulers · Putnam County haulers.

The same rural lot can need a different licensed trade. For nuisance wild animals, see Ohio wildlife control operators.
