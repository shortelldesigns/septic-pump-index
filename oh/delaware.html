Source retrieved 12 September 2026 (US/Pacific). Official file: HAULERS-JULY-2026.pdf. Parent page: Sewage. Verify statewide bonds via ODH Information for Contractors.

How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Delaware County septage-hauler registration only. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The PDF prints 36 TOTAL. Spellings are as printed (including truncated names CHUCKS…IN and UBERDROSS…REDBOX &, MASRYSVILLE Rd., and JEREMY HEAVEN). BLOODHOUND’s phone was printed as dashes and is marked unknown here.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Coshocton County haulers · Portage County haulers.
