How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is the Fairfield County sewage pumper/hauler roster only. The page title says these firms are licensed and bonded, but no per-firm bond number is printed, so ODH bond status is unknown. Operator, street, state, ZIP, and local REG # are unknown (Name, City, and Phone only).

The page prints a pill Revised: 08/12/26 and a caption Revised August 12, 2026. The HTML ships a placeholder 35 licensed & bonded pumpers/haulers; the page script replaces that count with the length of its firm array. That array, and a print of the rendered page, name 36 firms. We list all 36, in that order. Spellings are as printed, including Buds Septic Tank Cleaning (no apostrophe), Feecorp Corporation, Mt Gilead, Tidy Tims Inc, Sickels Sanitation, LLC, Affordable Portables after All-Star Septic, and The Waterworks after United Rentals (Reliable Onsite Services). Hanson Excavating DBA A-1 Septic Pumping is one row. Fairfield County Health Department: 1550 Sheridan Dr, Suite 100, Lancaster, Ohio 43130, phone (740) 652-2800.

A pumper/hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Pumping a tank is not an inspection. The same Sewage Treatment Systems page also links registered installers and service providers — those are separate lists and are not merged into this hauler table.
