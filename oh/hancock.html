How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Hancock County septage-hauler registration only. It is local registration, not the ODH statewide bond list. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF. The roster prints columns NAME, ADDRESS, CITY, Phone, and Alt Phone. It does not print operator names or local registration numbers, so those fields are omitted.

The PDF prints 11 named firms and no TOTAL line (footer updated 1/29/2026, page 1). This table has 11 rows. Spellings are as printed. STIGER PRECAST INC. keeps the period after INC. TE PRICE INC and RANDY MASTIN SEPTIC TANK INC have no period after INC. The printed name RANDY MASTIN SEPTIC TANK INC overflows the NAME column; the C of INC crosses the grid line into the ADDRESS cell, which prints 12004 WATERVILLE on the first line and SWANTON RD on the second. Those address lines are joined with a space. HOOVER SEPTIC TANK CLEANING and ALLEN HUGHES SEPTIC TANK SERVICE wrap inside the NAME column and are joined with a space. HORSES SANITATION SERVICE is printed without an apostrophe. DISTEL'S SEPTIC SERVICE keeps the apostrophe. RELIABLE ONSITE SERVICES is the NAME as printed (not United Rentals). The footer wraps the title as 2026 REGISTERED SEPTAGE WAISTE HAULERS (WAISTE as printed). Phones are shown as printed, including the Alt Phone values on TE PRICE INC and RELIABLE ONSITE SERVICES (the latter repeats 313-676-1083). Hancock Public Health: 2225 Keith Parkway, Findlay, Ohio 45840, phone 419-424-7105, fax 419-424-7872.

The Forms and Permits page also links 2026 Sewage System Designers & Soil Scientists, 2026 Licensed Sewage Treatment and Disposal Installers, 2026 Sewage Inspectors, and septic-registration form PDFs. This table does not transcribe those PDFs, and it does not add firms from the ODH bond list. The Hancock Public Health sewage page does not link this roster.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Williams County haulers · Putnam County haulers · Wyandot County haulers.
