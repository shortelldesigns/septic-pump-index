Portage County, Ohio — registered septage haulers

Transcribed from Portage County Combined General Health District’s PDF Septage Haulers, dated 17 July 2026 (2026-REGISTERED-SEPTAGE-HAULERS-07.17.26.pdf). Companies that pump septic tanks in Portage County must register with the health district. We did not add companies from business directories.

Source retrieved 4 September 2026 (US/Pacific). Official file: 2026-REGISTERED-SEPTAGE-HAULERS-07.17.26.pdf. Parent page: Contractors / Designers / Soil Scientists. Verify statewide bonds via ODH Information for Contractors.

How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Portage County registration only. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The PDF footer prints “43 TOTAL” on page 4 — we list all 43 named firms. Two business names are truncated on the source PDF (AUBURN BAINBRIDGE EXCAVATING; ADVANCED AER and MARK BISKUP DBA CHARLIE HOFFMAN SEPTIC SERVIC) and are kept as printed. Operator line for Bosley prints JOSHUA- PAUL TULLY (space after the hyphen) as on the PDF.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Geauga County pumpers · Lake County haulers.
