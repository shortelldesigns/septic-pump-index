Stark County, Ohio — registered septage haulers

Transcribed from Stark County Health Department’s PDF Septage Haulers (HDIS list, footer 5 March 2026, 36 TOTAL). Companies that haul septage in Stark County must register with the health department. We did not add companies from business directories.

Source retrieved 14 October 2026 (US/Pacific). Official file: 2026 HAULER LIST PDF. Parent page: Stark County sewage / environmental health. Verify statewide bonds via ODH Information for Contractors.

How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Stark County septage-hauler registration only. Local REG # and ODH bond status are unknown on this PDF (no registration numbers printed).

The PDF prints 36 TOTAL (footer 03/05/2026). Spellings are as printed (including JJC2, LTD DBA TOM'S SEPTIC AND SEPTIC, WERAB ENTERPRISES LLC D|B|A FREDS SEPTIC SERV, and ESZBIBB DBA COWBOY MILLER SEPTIC PUMPING INC). SPEEDIE SEPTIC & SEWER prints a blank operator line. Several phones print with a leading dash and no leading 1; GOLD-N-DEUCE LLC prints - - - (shown as unknown). The same Online Forms folder also hosts 2026 installer and service-provider lists — those are separate credentials and are not merged into this hauler table.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Brown County haulers · Summit County haulers.
