How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Van Wert County septage-hauler registration only. It is local registration, not the ODH statewide bond list. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The PDF prints 9 TOTAL (footer 06/09/2026, Page 1). Spellings are as printed. HORSES SANITATION SERVICE LLC has no apostrophe. GROTHOUSE PLBG. & HTG. INC. keeps the abbreviations and periods. GRIER'S POWER DIGGING LLC and MIKE'S SANITATION, INC. keep their apostrophes. BLUFFTON AERATION SERVICE, INC. keeps the comma and INC. FISCHER PLUMBING & HEATING, OHIO SANITARY SERVICES, and TRESSLER PLUMBING have no LLC suffix on this PDF. Street and city lines are joined with a space, as printed, with no added comma between them. No local registration numbers appear on this PDF. Phones are shown as printed apart from a stray leading hyphen the HDIS export adds on eight numbers (visible 419-692-8901 rather than -419-692-8901, and the same for the other seven). No digits were added or changed. Bluffton stays 1-419-358-9673. Van Wert County Health Department: 1179 WESTWOOD DRIVE, VAN WERT, OH 45891, phone 1-419-238-0808, fax 1-419-238-9571.

The Household Sewage Treatment Systems page also links Sewage Treatment System Installers and Service Providers. This table does not transcribe those PDFs, and it does not add firms from the ODH bond list.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Darke County haulers · Wyandot County haulers.

The same rural lot can need a different licensed trade. For nuisance wild animals, see Ohio wildlife control operators.
