Source retrieved 16 September 2026 (US/Pacific). Official file: 2026-Registered-Haulers-6.4.26.pdf. Downloads page: Household Sewage Downloads. Program page: Sewage Haulers, Installers and Service Providers. Verify statewide bonds via ODH Information for Contractors.

How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Cuyahoga County septage-hauler registration only. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The PDF lists 38 registered haulers (footer Last updated 6/3/2026). Spellings are as printed (including Geauga Septic Service Llc; P.O Box 301; Remson Rd). United Rentals DBA line is kept as printed (United Rentals (North America), Inc; DBA Reliable Onsite Services). No local registration numbers appear on this PDF. Fax and email columns from the PDF are omitted here.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Clark County haulers · Geauga County pumpers.
