Summit County, Ohio — registered septage haulers

Transcribed from Summit County Public Health’s printable PDF 2026 STS Registered Contractors List (Updated: 9/2/26). Companies that haul septage in Summit County must register with SCPH. We tabulated only rows marked Septage Hauler = Y. We did not add companies from business directories.

Source retrieved 17 September 2026 (US/Pacific). Official printable file: scph.link/STS_Contractors. Operation permits page: Registered Contractors. STS contractor page: STS Contractor. Verify statewide bonds via ODH Information for Contractors.

How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Summit County septage-hauler registration only. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The full contractors PDF lists installers, service providers, and haulers. We list the 33 rows with Septage Hauler = Y (Updated: 9/2/26). Street addresses are not on this PDF — City only. Spellings kept as printed (including POPS SEPTIC & DRAIN SERVICE double space; WERAB ENTERPRISES / FRED'S SEPTIC; SKELLEY SEPTIC & WELL INSPECTIONS; CLN PORTABLE RESTROOM).

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Cuyahoga County haulers · Portage County haulers.
