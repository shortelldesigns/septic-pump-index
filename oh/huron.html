How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Huron County STS hauler registration only. Local REG # and ODH bond status are unknown on this PDF (no registration numbers printed). Operator is unknown (business, city/ZIP, phone, and email only; street addresses are not printed).

The PDF prints Last Updated: 11.4.2026 and we list all 28 named rows. Spellings are as printed, including curly apostrophes and Kieth’s Drain & Septic Service. City and ZIP only (no street line). Emails on the PDF are not shown in this table. Huron County Public Health: 28 Executive Drive, Norwalk, OH 44857, phone 1-419-668-1652.
