Mahoning County, Ohio — registered septage haulers

Transcribed from Mahoning County Health Dept’s PDF Septage Haulers (footer 2 March 2026, 25 TOTAL). Companies that haul septage in Mahoning County must register with the health district. We did not add companies from business directories.

Source retrieved 23 October 2026 (US/Pacific). Official file: 2026 List of Registered Septage Haulers/Pumpers PDF. Parent page: Mahoning County Health septic program. Verify statewide bonds via ODH Information for Contractors.

How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Mahoning County septage-hauler registration only. Local REG # and ODH bond status are unknown on this PDF (no registration numbers printed).

The PDF prints 25 TOTAL (footer 03/02/2026). Spellings are as printed (including OBA where other county lists print DBA, LYNNVIZDOR, KINGSSVILLE, ST RT RT 88, and 38350ADAMS). MATT MORJOCK (NO TRUCK) is listed as printed. Mahoning County Health Dept: 50 Westchester Dr., Youngstown, OH 44515, phone 1-330-270-2855.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Columbiana County haulers · Stark County haulers.
