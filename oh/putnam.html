How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Putnam County septage-hauler registration only. It is local registration, not the ODH statewide bond list. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The PDF prints 16 TOTAL (footer 06/08/2026, Page 2). Spellings are as printed. ALL SERVICE AERATION, INC has no period after INC. The operator BUETTNER, JASON is last-name-first, as printed. HORSE'S SANITATION SERVICES LLC keeps the apostrophe and the plural SERVICES. FUSIONSITE OHIO LLC DBA C & L SANITATION and operator TOM & ROBIN L STANGE are as printed. The official PDF truncates the last firm name at UNITED RENTALS (NORTH AMERICA), INC.; DBA: RE; that exact string is kept. Street and city lines are joined with a space, as printed, with no added comma between them. No local registration numbers appear on this PDF. Phones are shown as printed apart from a stray leading hyphen the HDIS export adds on Brinkman Sanitation Rentals (visible 419-523-3726 rather than -419-523-3726). No digits were added or changed. Putnam County Health Department: 256 WILLIAMSTOWN ROAD, OTTAWA, OH 45875, phone 1-419-523-5608, fax 1-419-523-4171.

The Sewage page also links Putnam County Registered Septic System Installers and Putnam County Registered Septic System Service Providers. This table does not transcribe those PDFs, it does not use the older 28 October 2024 combined service-provider/hauler PDF still in the media library, and it does not add firms from the ODH bond list.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Van Wert County haulers · Wyandot County haulers.
