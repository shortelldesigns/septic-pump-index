How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Fulton County septage-hauler registration only. It is local registration, not the ODH statewide bond list. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The PDF prints 14 TOTAL (footer 08/10/2026, Page 2). Spellings are as printed. AUTOMATIC SEPTIC & WELL CORP. keeps the ampersand and the period after CORP.; the operator line is printed VICKIE PANT. KOOPS SEPTIC SERVICE LLC has no apostrophe. LAYMAN'S SEPTIC TANK CLEANING LLC and TRESSLER'S PLUMBING keep the apostrophes. T.E. PRICE INC keeps the periods in T.E. and has no period after INC. RANDY MASTIN SEPTIC TANK INC has no period after INC; the address is printed 12004 STATE ROUTE 64. PRECISION WELL AND SEPTIC LLC uses AND, not an ampersand, and has no comma before LLC; the city line is printed NAPOLEON, OH 43567. SEPTIC & SEWER SOLUTIONS uses an ampersand and has no LLC; the city line is printed NAPOLEON, OH 43545. UNITED RENTALS - RELIABLE ONSITE SERVICES keeps the hyphen; the second line is printed DBA: RELIABLE ONSITE SERVICES and is kept in the operator column. The operator on C & L SANITATION is printed TOM STANGE GM. Street and city lines are joined with a space, as printed, with no added comma between them. No local registration numbers appear on this PDF. Phones are shown as printed, including the leading 1-. Fulton County Health Department: 606 S SHOOP AVE, WAUSEON, OH 43567, phone 1-419-337-0915, fax 1-419-337-0561.

The HSTS/Sewage page also links Installer (INSTALLER_LIST_08.2026.pdf) and Service Provider (SERVICE_PROVIDER_LIST_08.2026.pdf) PDFs. This table does not transcribe those PDFs, and it does not add firms from the ODH bond list.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Williams County haulers · Van Wert County haulers · Hancock County haulers.
