How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Richland County septage-hauler registration only. Local REG # and ODH bond status are unknown on this PDF (no registration numbers printed).

The PDF prints 22 TOTAL and we list all 22 rows. Three rows print no phone (the two Oyster’s land-application rows for Matt and Chris Oyster, and John R Horning for Rick Bond); those are marked unknown. Spellings are as printed, including MLLER, WEBBS, ADARIO, and the truncated DBA RELI on the United Rentals row. Richland Public Health: 555 Lexington Ave, Mansfield, OH 44907, phone 1-419-774-4500.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Pumping a tank is not an inspection. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Ashland County haulers · Marion County haulers.
