How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Tuscarawas County septage-hauler registration only. Local REG # and ODH bond status are unknown on this PDF (no registration numbers printed). Operator is unknown (name, address, and phone only).

The PDF footer prints Last Updated: 01/21/2026 NB and we list all 10 named rows. Spellings are as printed, including Carrolton, Kline's Environment Service, Shetler Services Inc, and Speedie’s mailing: P.O. Box 167. Tuscarawas County Health Department: 897 E. Iron Ave., Dover, OH 44622, phone 1-330-343-5555.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Pumping a tank is not an inspection. The same parent page also links a 2026 Septic Service Providers list — that is a separate credential and is not merged into this hauler table.
