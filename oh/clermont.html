How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Clermont County septage-hauler registration only. Local REG # and ODH bond status are unknown on this PDF (no registration numbers printed).
