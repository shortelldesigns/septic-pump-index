Butler County, Ohio — registered septage haulers

Transcribed from Butler County Health District’s PDF Registered Professionals 7-23-26 (dated 23 July 2026). Companies that haul septage in Butler County must register with the health district. We tabulated only the SEPTAGE HAULER section. We did not add companies from business directories.

Source retrieved 18 September 2026 (US/Pacific). Official file: Registered Professionals 7-23-26.pdf. Sewage & private water page: Sewage and Private Water. Forms listing: Environmental Health forms. Verify statewide bonds via ODH Information for Contractors.

How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Butler County septage-hauler registration only. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF (the Bond Company column is not ODH verification).

The PDF lists 25 SEPTAGE HAULER rows (filename dated 7-23-26). SERVICE PROVIDER and INSTALLER sections are omitted. Spellings kept as printed (including LANSCAPING; UNITED RENTALS ( NORTH AMERICIA); Attn: Melissa). EXNIL GREASE LLC and TRENTELS have no phone on the PDF. EXNIL hauler address prints Cincinnati with no ZIP. Rumpke phone kept as printed with X7015.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Summit County haulers · Clark County haulers.
