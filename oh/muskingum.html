Muskingum County, Ohio — registered septage haulers

Transcribed from Zanesville-Muskingum County Health Department’s PDF 2026 Septage Haulers (Sewage-Haulers.pdf, footer 24 March 2026; expires 31 December 2026). Companies that haul septage in Muskingum County must register with the health department. We did not add companies from business directories.

Source retrieved 20 September 2026 (US/Pacific). Official file: Sewage-Haulers.pdf. Parent page: Sewage Treatment Systems. Verify statewide bonds via ODH Information for Contractors.

How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Muskingum County septage-hauler registration only. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The PDF lists 19 named haulers (footer DP 3/24/26; expires 12/31/2026). Spellings are as printed (including curly apostrophes in Big Al’s / Jack’s, Jack’s … & Const truncation, and Ohio vs OH in city lines). Registration numbers are the local ZMCHD numbers from the PDF. Some PDF name lines carry trailing * or ** role markers (footer: ** can install, inspect and pump; the PDF also prints two different single-* legends). Markers are not part of the business name here; this page is the septage-hauler roster only.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Coshocton County haulers · Licking County haulers.
