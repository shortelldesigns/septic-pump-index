How to read the table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Geauga registration only. Contact person and ODH bond are unknown on this PDF. Verify bonds via ODH Information for Contractors.

Point-of-sale inspections are a service provider registration in Ohio, not the hauler category. Repair is an installer category.
