Transcribed from Columbiana County General Health District’s PDF 2026 STS Pumpers Alphabetical by City (2026-STS-Pumpers.pdf, PDF dated 22 April 2026). Companies that pump septic tanks in Columbiana County must register with the health district. We did not add companies from business directories.

Source retrieved 10 September 2026 (US/Pacific). Official file: 2026-STS-Pumpers.pdf. Parent page: Sewage. Verify statewide bonds via ODH Information for Contractors.

How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Columbiana County STS pumper registration only. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The PDF lists 22 pumpers alphabetical by city. Spellings are as printed (including Willam Milner, Charlie Hoffman Septic Svcs., N. Benton, and Yo-town Kingsville).

A septage pumper registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Portage County haulers · Lake County haulers.
