How to read this table

Ohio requires registration with each local health district (ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03) plus a statewide surety bond at the Ohio Department of Health. This table is Greene County septage-hauler registration only. It is local registration, not the ODH statewide bond list. ODH bond status is unknown on this PDF.

The PDF prints 12 TOTAL (footer 07/28/2026, Page 2). Spellings are as printed. BLUE OX PORTABLE SERVICES, LLC (operator JARRETT TEST/WHITNEY MOORE) and BLUE OX SEPTIC SERVICES (operator JARRETT TEST) are two separate firms; both are kept. JOHNS RELIABLE SEPTIC has no apostrophe. C T BROWN SANITATION keeps the spaces between C, T, and BROWN. TRENTEL'S PORTABLES keeps the apostrophe. Street and city lines are joined with a space, as printed, with no added comma between them. No local registration numbers appear on this PDF. Greene County Public Health: 360 WILSON DRIVE, XENIA, OH 45385-1810, phone 1-937-374-5661, fax 1-937-374-5675.

The On-site Sewage Systems page also links Registered Installers and Registered Service Providers. This table does not transcribe those PDFs, and it does not add firms from the ODH bond list.

A septage hauler registration is not a service-provider inspection credential. Point-of-sale inspections are a separate registration category in Ohio. How often to pump · Inspection before sale · Clark County haulers · Butler County haulers.
