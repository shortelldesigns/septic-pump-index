Official tools for preferred states

Pennsylvania

Anyone who pumps septic tanks or hauls residential septage must register with DEP. DEP issues a 5-digit transporter number. Register a Residential Septage Hauler. DEP’s “Active Residential Septage Haulers” spreadsheet (April 2024 filename) failed with a TLS error from this environment.

Chester County Health Department licenses liquid waste pumpers and publishes a dated PDF roster. Official list: Liquid Waste Pumpers (last revised 28 July 2026). Parent: Licensed Contractors in Chester County. Sewage program: Sewage & Water.

Ohio

ORC 3718 / OAC 3701-29-03: register as installer, service provider, and/or septage hauler with each local health district. Statewide $25,000 septage-hauler bond is filed with ODH. ODH Information for Contractors. Lake County list: HAULER-030326.pdf (3 March 2026). Parent: LCGHD O&M program. Portage County list: 2026-REGISTERED-SEPTAGE-HAULERS-07.17.26.pdf (17 July 2026). Parent: Portage contractors / designers. Columbiana County list: 2026-STS-Pumpers.pdf (PDF dated 22 April 2026). Parent: Columbiana Sewage. Coshocton County list: Septic-Haulers-Installers-Service-Providers-2026.pdf (PDF dated 29 May 2026; hauler rows only on our page). Parent: Coshocton Private Sewage. Delaware County list: HAULERS-JULY-2026.pdf (footer 21 July 2026). Parent: Delaware Public Health District Sewage. Marion County list: Marion-County-Registered-Septage-Haulers-2026.06.16.pdf (updated 16 June 2026). Parent: Marion Public Health HSTS. Licking County list: sewage-treatment-haulers-02-12-2026.pdf (footer 12 February 2026; 34 TOTAL). Parent: Licking County Sewage Treatment Program.

Wisconsin

Septage businesses are licensed under ch. NR 113, Wis. Adm. Code. Search active licenses from Septage business license requirements. A DNR PDF roster we fetched is dated 30 June 2023; we did not transcribe it as current.

Iowa

Septic tanks must be pumped only by a licensed commercial septic tank cleaner (IAC 567 Chapter 68). Current roster: Licensed Iowa Septic Tank Cleaners PDF. Program: Septic Tank Cleaning. Time of Transfer inspectors are a separate DNR certification.

Minnesota

SSTS businesses are licensed by the Minnesota Pollution Control Agency. Benton County publishes a local list of designers, inspectors, installers, maintainers, and service providers. Maintainer = pumper. Official PDF: DocumentCenter/View/238. Parent: Benton County SSTS. Verify: MPCA SSTS business licensing.

Illinois

McLean County Health Department licenses septic system pumpers and publishes a dated PDF roster. Official list: 0601-30-Pumper-list-2026 (revised 12 May 2026). Parent: Septic Systems Contractor Information. Installers are a separate PDF. Statewide context: Illinois Department of Public Health.

Kansas

Johnson County Department of Health and Environment licenses sanitary disposal contractors (pumpers) under the county Environmental Sanitary Code. Official sewage list: 2026 Licensed Sanitary Disposal Contractors Lists.pdf (updated 24 June 2026). Parent: Pumper Installer Lists. Licensing: Contractor Installer Licensing.

Michigan

Licensing is under Part 117 of NREPA, administered by EGLE. We did not transcribe names. Use the program page above until we can fetch a dated public list.

How other states will be added

EPA does not keep a national database of septic systems; they are regulated by states, tribes, and local governments (EPA FAQ). We add a county or state page only after we fetch an official HTML table, PDF, or spreadsheet and transcribe it. See methodology and README.
